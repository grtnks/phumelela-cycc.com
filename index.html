About Phumelela CYCC

Phumelela Child and Youth Care Centre (Phumelela CYCC) intends to operate as a Non-Profit Organisation (NPO) and obtain Public Benefit Organisation (PBO) status.

Phumelela aims to establish a safe and nurturing environment that supports the growth and well-being of children and youth in every aspect of their lives.

The organisation intends to uplift children and youth by helping them discover themselves in Jesus Christ, addressing social challenges that limit their growth and opportunities, and providing resources and mentorship to build their confidence and self-esteem. Through transforming mindsets and creating pathways to new opportunities, Phumelela aims to support a brighter and more hopeful future.

Our Purpose

Our Vision and Mission

Our Vision

To establish a safe and nurturing environment that is sustainable for the growth and well-being of children and youth in every aspect of their lives.

Our Mission

The organisation intends to uplift children and youth by first and foremost helping them discover themselves in Jesus Christ, addressing social challenges that limit their growth and opportunities, providing resources and mentorship to build their confidence and self-esteem, transforming mindsets, and creating pathways toward a brighter and more hopeful future.

How We Make a Difference

Our Services

Phumelela aims to support the overall development, safety, and well-being of children and youth through the following services.

Safe Residential Care

Providing a safe, caring, and supportive living environment for children and youth.

Education

Promoting education, encouraging learning, and supporting educational development.

Health and Well-being

Improving health and promoting the physical and overall well-being of children and youth.

Emotional and Spiritual Support

Supporting emotional growth, spiritual development, and personal well-being.

Life Skills

Helping children and youth develop essential life skills for greater independence and confidence.

Family and Community

Strengthening family relationships and building positive connections within the community.

Children's Rights

Promoting and protecting the rights, dignity, and safety of every child.

Social Integration

Encouraging positive relationships and helping children and youth participate in their communities.

Our Future

Building a Foundation for the Future
